## 1. Area summary

`src/lib/desktop` has 51 files. About 16.6K lines are product code (1.2K of that is two browser test harnesses) and about 28.7K lines are tests. `native-playback-controller.test.ts` alone is 19,741 lines with 436 tests.
It is the renderer half of the macOS desktop app. It holds two things:

1. **Native playback.** A renderer-side controller drives the native C++ audio host over `window.dawDesktop.audioHost.session`. Modules:
   - `native-playback-controller.ts` is the orchestrator. It owns about 80 closure variables, the transport lane, start/pause/seek/loop, prepare, adoption and publication.
   - `native-processor-state-owner.ts` is the ledger of desired and installed processor content. It owns execution records, receipts, fences and the adoption/publication records.
   - `native-publication-transaction.ts` is the proof loop (`runPublication`).
   - `native-schedule-publication.ts` is the schedule strategy for that loop.
   - `native-schedule-coordinator.ts` compiles and refills schedule windows.
   - `native-timeline-page-manager.ts` hydrates PCM pages into the host.
   - Slices cut out of the controller: `native-retirement`, `native-state-patches`, `native-parameter-events`, `native-live-midi`, `native-observation`, `native-recording-session`, `native-host-connection-loss`, `native-graph-targets`, `native-snapshot-support`.
   - Pure helpers: `native-built-in-parameter-mapper` (effect edit to native values/bytes), `native-processor-automation`, `native-external-attachment-plan`, `native-instrument-budget-policy`.
2. **Attached host.** `attached-host-controller.ts` is the renderer end of the desktop control protocol (`host.*`, `control.*`, `transport.*`, `diagnostics.*`). It uses `import-job-queue`, `capability-file`, `capability-export-output-targets` and `native-diagnostics-serialization`.
3. **Small VST-side modules.** `native-vst3-insertion`, `vst3-catalog-auto-heal`, `vst-parameter-feedback-controller`, `native-vst-parameter-queue`, `native-vst-state-capture`, `vst-instance-failures` (module-global Solid signals).
4. **Test harnesses.** `native-playback-lifecycle.browser-runtime.ts` and `native-playback-timeline.browser-runtime.tsx` are browser-bundle harnesses that live beside product code.

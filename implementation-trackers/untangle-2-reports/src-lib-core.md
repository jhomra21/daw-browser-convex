## 1. Area summary

`src/lib` top level is a flat bag of 165 non-test modules, grouped only by file-name prefix: `timeline-*` 26, `local-*` 18, `clip-*` 12, `audio-*` 12, `track-*` 7, `project-*` 7, waveform/sample/spectrum 14, `external-*` 4, `cloud-*` 4, sampled-instrument 4. Subfolders (desktop, export, undo, timeline-repository, recording, midi, local-control) hold the rest of lib. What the top level owns:
- Local-first persistence on IndexedDB/OPFS: `local-project-db.ts` (872 lines, catalog, per-project DB, deletion wiring), `local-project-deletion.ts`, `local-project-asset-lock.ts`, `local-assets.ts`, `local-effects.ts`, `local-automation.ts`, `external-plugins.ts`, `local-write-queue.ts`, `local-cloud-id-map.ts`, project archive/manifest, `cloud-backup.ts`.
- Cloud sharing writes: `shared-outbox.ts` (1721 lines, durable queue, claims, retries, completions, plus history-update bus), `timeline-clip-write-adapter.ts`, `timeline-track-write-adapter.ts`, `clip-mutations.ts`, `*-mutation-args.ts`, `shared-timeline-operations-api.ts`, `resumable-audio-uploader.ts`.
- Playback: `portable-browser-playback-controller.ts` (3399 lines), `portable-frame-schedule.ts`, `portable-live-session.ts`, `live-playback-snapshot.ts`, `desired-playback-state.ts`, `live-processor-control.ts`, `processor-edit-semantics.ts`, sampled-instrument loaders (`sampler-buffer-sync.ts` 1276, `drum-rack-buffer-sync.ts` 772, `sampled-instrument-region-budget.ts` 640, region loader, session).
- Effects domain: `audio-effect-kinds.ts`, `audio-effect-kind.ts`, `track-state-snapshot.ts`, `audio-effect-chain-presets.ts`, `device-catalog.ts`, `mixed-effect-order.ts`.
- Timeline pure helpers (geometry, layout, selection, section edit, drag placement/commit/persistence, track group ops, audio import, clip create) and UI-adjacent helpers (keyboard/pointer targets, waveform raster/layout, spectrum render, preferences).
Main dependencies out: `@daw-browser/{shared,timeline-core,audio-engine,control}`, `~/lib/{undo,desktop,export,timeline-repository,recording,local-control}`, and (wrongly, see N1) hooks/components. In: almost only hooks (single consumer for 37 files) and components. `audio-engine-singleton.ts` and `convex.ts` are process-wide singletons.
